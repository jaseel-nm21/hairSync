How HairSync Operates

A clear, transparent journey from donor cutting chair to recipient smile.

1
Sign Up & Profile

Donors and recipients set up authenticated profiles with specific requirements and health details.

2
NGO Verification

Platform administrators strictly verify NGO credentials, non-profit legitimacy, and registration papers.

3
Donation Collection

Verified collection drives, length measurements, and hygienic storage prepare hair for wig craftsmen.

4
Wig Delivery

Handcrafted natural hair wigs are allocated and tracked straight to recipient hands with full transparency.

About The Project

Empowering Cancer & Alopecia Warriors

Hair loss caused by chemotherapy, radiation, or chronic autoimmune conditions like Alopecia can profoundly impact a patient's self-esteem and mental well-being during their hardest battle.

While millions of generous donors are willing to cut their locks, traditional donation methods rely on informal social media calls, non-standardized shipments, and lack of follow-up.

HairSync solves this via centralized digital identity, role-based workflows, and systematic inventory coordination across donors, NGOs, and medical patients.
